# Maintainer guidance

When auditing new testing-center results for Proof Graphify or Proof Check, consult [the effectiveness tracker](architecture/effectiveness-tracker.md). Update only the entries exercised by evidence already examined in that audit, preserving earlier observations and limitations.

This is maintenance tracking, not a new paper-analysis requirement. Do not add review rounds, source passes or test runs just to populate it, and do not treat an unproven benefit as authorization to remove an instruction.
