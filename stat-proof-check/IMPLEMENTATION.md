## Common contract

The [shared-core and reader handoff](../architecture/shared-core-reader-handoff-20261009.md) defines the 3.1.0 integration. Both products use one math implementation and scientific reader, with existing prepared-content adapters retaining their distinct depth. Audit evidence remains pinned; overview edits preserve historical examinations and their audit-only fields while normal bindings determine current freshness. Primary work, same-model independent commissioning, integration and frozen publication retain the v3 scientific contract below.

- Reuse composition checks for coherent written proof units and statement-only assessment of a manuscript proof omission. Statement-only assessments do not create a proof route or establish the theorem. Use `extent` (`whole_unit` or `partial`) and `omissions` (text). The record schemas allow these optional fields for reading history, but a complete composition under the new protocol requires an explicit extent; partial requires nonblank omissions. Worker judgment and submission formats carry these fields unchanged. Other check kinds may omit them.
- Qualification IDs in response/submission envelopes can be null under the new protocol. Do not create fake calibration. Preserve real response identity, original bytes, exposure and source-only review. Existing legacy review consumers retain their historical qualification meaning.
- Full/focused audits may use an empty `global_tasks` array under the new protocol. Cross-proof questions normally belong to the consuming proof's examinations. Explicitly selected additional primary inspection belongs in Stage 1, with integration in Stage 2 and actual review coverage disclosed; do not manufacture three not-applicable global rows.
- Required units derive from declared major results, their necessary supplied-paper prerequisites in Focused scope, and meaningful explicitly selected subsections, not each intermediate record. Reuse existing audit targets and arguments; do not add another task registry.
- A whole-unit supported composition provides the local reasoning basis only within its examined unit, using the primary judgment or the saved judgment adopted during integration. Suppliers, AND/OR, scope/discharge, cycles, local defects and independent findings still determine support. Partial/gap/inconclusive units provide no blanket positive basis. Optional local checks can preserve a good internal inference in a failed proof, without a new worker assignment.
- No required character coverage ledger. Preserve located proof boundaries and continuations, meaningful reasoning, actual extent and omissions.
- One integration per unit includes its mapped local review opinions and may end with unresolved scientific disagreement. Adopting a saved whole-unit judgment requires no new examination and preserves the original opinions. Performed work and favorable outcome differ. An assessed proof omission or inaccessible external citation can finish uncertain. Missing promised work or unexamined supplied material remains incomplete and yields working output.
- Stage 3 takes only a frozen bundle and output path, with no live database derivation.
