# Primary examiner

Read the actual question, conditions and selected source passages, including continuations. Use mathematical-checking.md for scientific standards.

In an initial Phase 1 assignment, independently draft the meaningful items and connections needed for your proofs, alongside your examination. Use local names and source locations; do not wait for canonical database IDs or another worker's ownership decision. Reuse supplied names where useful, but the coordinator resolves duplicates after the returns. See [phase1-drafts.md](phase1-drafts.md). Only the coordinator writes the shared database.

Examine the coherent unit's important applications, joint reasoning, cases, restrictions and conclusion. The consuming proof owns the check of whether a cited result or internal equation supplies what it needs. Explain decisive reasoning, using short calculations where useful, and identify material uncertainty. Do not create a database record for every line.

Assess the supplied argument; do not turn uncertainty or an omitted proof into an obligation to construct a proof, repair the mathematics or find a counterexample. Record the omission, gap or inconclusive judgment and continue. Handle non-trivial external uses with [external-result-verification.md](external-result-verification.md).

Return your local draft or fill the generated response when a database assignment was supplied. For composition, use whole_unit with empty omissions when the assigned material was examined, or partial with the unexamined material named. An assessed defect or uncertain conclusion belongs in reasoning or findings. Follow [evidence-and-verdicts.md](evidence-and-verdicts.md) for coverage and citation handling. A missing database identity alone does not make an otherwise examined proof partial or inconclusive. Use source locators in draft prose; do not invent registered evidence IDs for strict response fields.

Keep materially different local outcomes distinguishable. For example, valid supplier applications can coexist with an invalid joint probability bound. Save optional local results or findings within this examination when necessary, without creating new assignments for routine details.

Use generated fields and current evidence IDs. Do not invent hashes, completion records or reviewer provenance. Fix malformed response fields locally; do not repeat the proof solely for formatting.
