# Additional supplied-route review


Preserve the initial source-only response before exposing any primary reconstruction. Routine
details independently reconstructed and actually examined in that response can receive credit through
transparent exact mapping and reconciliation. New intermediate IDs or a reconstruction origin alone
do not require another call.

If support relies on substantive new mathematics or changed conditions the reviewer never examined,
review that specific route, its exact target, inputs, scope, and supplied derivation in a fresh context
without primary verdicts. Identify the dispatch as `route_provided`, not `source_only`, and preserve
both responses with their actual scope. Supplied-route review cannot replace blind review of the
written proof or silently extend an earlier judgment to an unexamined repair. Use
`work prepare --mode independent --route arg_ID` as described in
[controller-workflow.md](controller-workflow.md); do not smuggle supplied reasoning into a source-only packet.
