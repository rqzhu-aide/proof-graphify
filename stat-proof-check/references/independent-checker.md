# Independent examiner

For an ordinary written proof, work from the delivered source-only packet, its exact neutral question and source passages. The owning theorem may supply context; it does not replace a local assigned conclusion. Preserve the supplied question_id. An explicitly supplied-route assignment instead asks you to evaluate the visible proposed route and records route_provided exposure.

A reviewer group can contain several ordinary packets. Examine them in the same independent source context if useful and save a separate response for each packet. Return completed responses even if another packet needs context. Do not write the shared database, read coordinator-only manifests or recruit additional reviewers as an assumed duty.

Examine the assigned mathematical question under mathematical-checking.md and [evidence-and-verdicts.md](evidence-and-verdicts.md). Put concerns about the assigned conclusion in judgment reasoning and outcome, and other concerns in coverage_note.

Include the important connections assigned to the consuming proof. Use [external-result-verification.md](external-result-verification.md) for non-trivial external applications.

Use the [research-severity rubric](evidence-and-verdicts.md#research-severity) when describing consequence and apparent repairability. No separate severity opinion is required in a source-only response; the coordinator creates formal findings during integration.

For source-only review, do not consult primary judgments or suggested solutions before the initial response. Matching original sources, source-only page images and role references are permitted. The generated source-navigation.json locates packet passages across sources, including prerequisites and continuations; it creates no additional examination requirement. If a consequential symbol is unreadable, identify the affected step and request that passage or retain the limitation; do not infer it from corrupted extraction. In `exposure_report`, `none_known` means no known exposure to disallowed mathematical opinions, not verified isolation. Neutral file names, paths, identifiers and administrative status with no mathematical judgment do not by themselves change that status. Use `possible_exposure` when content actually encountered contains or may contain disallowed opinions, including judgment-bearing names, and explain the specific uncertainty in the existing note. Source-only continuation may reuse the same reviewer's source-only work or its explicitly assigned predecessor's original source-only work, identified by the existing handoff note. That reuse alone is permitted, including in a fresh same-model context. For source-only work, primary opinions remain disallowed; other reviewers' judgments are disallowed outside that assigned lineage. A continuation label cannot clear real exposure. A declared supplied route remains permitted, without becoming source-only review. Never claim source-only independence for a shared primary context. Ordinary v3 examination requires no calibration. Commissioning follows the coordinator's [same-model policy](controller-workflow.md#stage-2-review-and-integrate), which is usable with any provider.

Fill the generated response-scaffold.json using worker-guidance.json, keeping its fields and question_id. Save it at the assigned response path; if you cannot write there, return the same filled scaffold through stdout or the individual return channel for the coordinator to capture. If the source is incomplete, identify the affected inference rather than silently broadening or narrowing the assignment. A useful adverse observation outside the requested scope should remain explicit; it is not a reason to recheck the whole paper.

For a newly noticed dependency, give its source location and consequence in reasoning or coverage notes. Missing graph bookkeeping is not itself a mathematical gap. The coordinator resolves shared identities and provides needed neutral source context; continue unaffected work. A materially changed question or newly needed evidence calls for only affected continuation, reusing current reasoning. Unrelated discoveries do not expand the assignment.

The coordinator integrates the saved response. Do not repeatedly review unchanged mathematics merely to obtain agreement or satisfy record formatting.
