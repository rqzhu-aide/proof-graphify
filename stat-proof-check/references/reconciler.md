# Integrate a proof examination

The coordinator owns integration. Use Stage 2 integration preparation and its generated response. Read the saved primary and independent reasoning with the exact source question; preserve each examiner's position on disputed points and distinguish the coordinator's decision from reviewer agreement.

Integrate each disputed or newly added concern from the saved reasoning under the exact question's applicable hypotheses, definitions, regime and their immediately apparent consequences. A surviving downstream conclusion does not establish a disputed intermediate assertion or erase an established local defect. Use [proportionate attention](mathematical-checking.md#proportionate-attention) only for a decisive point that remains unsettled; do not repeat decisive reasoning already sufficient for this decision merely to assign severity. Settle a clear minor correction from that reasoning and continue. For a potentially severe concern, address the concrete reason it may fail or materially restrict the result before adopting it; severity alone creates no new reviewer or review round. Explain acceptance, rejection or uncertainty before selecting the whole-unit judgment. Agreement is not required. When resolving an encountered discrepancy, use the relevant source and definitions, reusing adequate evidence already inspected. Preserve mathematical scope: for example, distinguish \(\frac{a+b}{c}\) from \(a+\frac{b}{c}\), \(\beta^{-1}/2\) from \(\beta^{-1/2}\), \(\sqrt[4]{r}\) from \(4\sqrt r\), and \(n^{-1}\sum_i\) from \(n^{-1/2}\sum_i\); for finite sums, \(\max_j\sum_i a_{ij}\le\sum_i\max_j a_{ij}\), not generally the reverse. Saved labels or reviewer agreement do not settle a discrepancy; the examiner may also be wrong. Correct mistranscriptions in the authoritative saved source representation; preserve faithful source text and record established source defects separately. A concern whose validity depends on unresolved source interpretation remains uncertainty rather than an established defect. Resolve only the affected point within the existing review, without reopening unaffected completed work. A concern first noticed here may remain a finding or uncertainty; reflect its consequence in the affected conclusions without automatically reopening related proofs.

When integration establishes a new material finding, save it once in the response's existing top-level `findings`, using the canonical [research-severity rubric](evidence-and-verdicts.md#research-severity) and concise [existing fields](reporting-and-release.md#concise-issue-cards). Do not duplicate an existing finding for the same concern; a `gap` or unresolved decision alone need not create one. Set severity when the saved reasoning permits; omission is unrated and does not block delivery. Resolve severity-label disagreements from that reasoning without another referee, calibration round or repair search. A disputed finding's validity remains neutral; retain a separate established issue's rating alongside unrelated uncertainty. An obvious one-line correction creates no replacement route or repair review. Distinguish reliably known mathematics from an inspected external publication, with no mandatory retrieval.

For the saved whole-unit opinions, use adopt_independent to adopt the independent conclusion, adopt_primary to adopt the primary conclusion, agree for compatible opinions, or unresolved when the disagreement remains. Leave successor_checks empty or omitted with either adopt choice; scripts attach the selected saved checks. Existing primary_revised and independent_revised inputs remain supported for compatibility and advanced explicit selection. Matching whole-unit outcomes do not settle a disputed intermediate assertion or dismiss its finding.

When adopted reasoning resolves or changes an identified finding, update only the fields whose content changed: `description`, `impact_reason`, severity, target, affected_uses, lifecycle/resolution or category. Carry the adopted qualification into the card; do not leave an unconditional criticism or claimed repair after accepting only a conditional argument. Keep a surviving defect on its affected assertion; `check_refs` preserves examination evidence without broadening its impact. A rationale alone does not update the finding. Carry the same correction into already identified current integration rationales and conclusions that depend on it, using the existing `--correct --task TASK_ID` route below for completed comparisons and reusing saved examinations. Retain independent surviving grounds; change a conclusion only as supported by those examinations. Preserve unchanged findings, unrelated concerns, original primary and independent opinions, and superseded history; these need not be rewritten. Uncertainty may remain open. This is limited to known affected records, without a graph sweep, duplicate finding, new reviewer or reopening unrelated proofs. Material changes to the claim, conditions or evidence still follow the affected-work rules below.

For that local update, obtain a current `get audit.db --target items:OWNER_ID --mode reconcile --out finding-context.json` packet, using the owning result from the graph (or `parts:PART_ID` for a result part). This command accepts result targets, not argument, group or use IDs; do not infer an owner by changing an ID prefix. Use `template ... --collection findings --id FINDING_ID --packet PACKET_ID --out finding-edit.json` for the current body, edit only the intended fields, and submit its `template` member with `review reconcile audit.db --batch BATCH.json`. Use lifecycle `resolved` with a resolution when the concern is settled, or retain an open `presentation` concern when only wording remains.

Correct material errors in the authoritative saved source representation; describing a correction only in review prose does not update it. Preserve original responses. When affected work needs a current binding but the exact mathematical question and inspected evidence are unchanged, reuse the examiner's applicable reasoning in a corrected response naming the current packet, with an original-to-current intake rationale and the actual reviewer, exposure and coverage preserved. Record currentness alone requires no new reviewer or proof examination. If the saved claim, conditions or needed evidence materially change, reuse completed reasoning and examine only changed or still unexamined assigned content; report coverage of the current question from the combined examination, or retain the verification limitation. Merely replacing a packet ID does not establish support for changed content. Administrative mapping and equivalent explanatory detail require no new examination.

Keep distinct local outcomes and all relevant adverse concerns. Valid separable returned work remains useful when another row needs local repair. When the scaffold lists response_dispositions, explain how the preserved extra rows are handled without rewriting the reviewer response. Potentially material content remains a limitation until incorporated or explicitly resolved in the integration reasoning.

Scripts select current references. Original response bytes and previous judgments remain historical evidence. Do not manually enumerate all superseded checks or create clean-agreement rows for every optional detail.

To correct a completed comparison without changing its scientific inputs, use `stage2 prepare --mode integration --correct --task TASK_ID`, then submit its generated response normally. See [controller-workflow.md](controller-workflow.md) for partial selection and recovery. Only requested comparisons are revised.

An unresolved scientific conclusion, assessed proof omission or unavailable external source can be fully examined and integrated. Record usable partial returns with their omissions, but submit only integration tasks actually exported as ready. The unresolved decision does not waive missing required examination or unread supplied material.
