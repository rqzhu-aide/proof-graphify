## Implemented behavior

- Three stages: evidence and primary examination; independent review, integration and finalization; rendering from the frozen result.
- One normal examination per written proof or meaningful selected subsection. Routine graph detail creates no compulsory per-edge work, character ledger or calibration exercise.
- Full scope retains required proofs and missing sources. A completed gap, inconclusive examination or integrated disagreement can finish the work; missing promised examination or review cannot.
- Source questions preserve exact hypotheses, supplier statements, multiple files, selected passages and continuations. Independent questions expose the actual assigned claim without primary judgments.
- Scripts generate graph record pairs, response envelopes and reconciliation references. Valid separable review work survives a malformed extra row; integration can record its explicit disposition without changing original reviewer bytes or inventing independent evidence.
- Material changes to examined applications, inference structure, scope or source renew affected work. Cosmetic changes and equivalent explanatory detail do not automatically expand the audit.
- Frozen rendering does not read or mutate the live database. A failed presentation leaves the scientific result available.

The database keeps the baseline storage format and collections. No new database tables or collections were added. Small record extensions describe examination extent, source selections and disposition of preserved response rows. Historical stores retain their protocol; the v3 writer refuses to silently recertify them.
