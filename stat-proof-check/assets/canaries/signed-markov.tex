\begin{lemma}\label{lem:signed-markov}
Let $(X_n)_{n \ge 1}$ be real random variables with $E[X_n] \to 0$ as
$n \to \infty$. Then $X_n \to 0$ in probability.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Fix $\varepsilon > 0$.
By Markov's inequality, $P(|X_n| > \varepsilon) \le E[X_n] / \varepsilon$.
The right-hand side tends to zero by hypothesis, so
$P(|X_n| > \varepsilon) \to 0$.
Since $\varepsilon > 0$ was arbitrary, $X_n \to 0$ in probability.
\end{proof}
