## Preserve demonstrated gains and separate harness work

Search/owner navigation, compact cards, targeted valid-TeX rendering, source-evidence links and faithful saved-state projection have concrete benefits. Keep those benefits distinct from whether agents author correct mathematics or obey delivery instructions. Do not roll them back because an instruction has not yet proved useful.

Harness-imposed second-map reconstruction, invocation errors, duplicate launches, source-access failures, archive links and post-completion process delays belong to the testing harness record. Note their effects when encountered, but do not compensate by expanding scientific skill requirements. [Evidence and ownership](../../audit-reports/longitudinal-performance-20261028/summary.txt)

## Later cleanup decisions

Consider consolidating or removing marginal wording when relevant observations support little added benefit or repeated overhead, while retaining its necessary underlying contract. Consider retaining or narrowing a change when its benefit appears only in a particular setting. Where evidence is insufficient, leave the item unproven. No automatic rollback, additional review gate, fixed run count or background testing is required.

Record a future cleanup decision with its evidence and the exact affected instruction or mechanism. This register currently authorizes no cleanup or runtime change.
